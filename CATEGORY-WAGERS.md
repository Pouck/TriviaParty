# Category wagers

At each category introduction, the host can choose **PROPOSER UNE MISE**, or start normally without wagering. Players choose an integer stake with a slider; releasing it saves the stake. **MISER 0** records an explicit zero stake. Players can change their choice until the host selects **FERMER LES MISES ET COMMENCER**. Unsubmitted stakes default to zero.

Positive-score teams can stake from zero to their current score. Teams at zero or below can stake up to the highest base reward of the forthcoming category's remaining questions. Limits refresh while wagering is open, including after a score correction or a new team joins.

Stakes apply only to the first question. A correct answer earns the full stake in addition to ordinary question points. A wrong or unanswered response loses the full stake. Stakes do not decrease with the question timer. Scores may become negative. In buzzer questions, teams without a correct answer also lose their stakes.

After answer reveal, the host's next action opens **RÉSULTATS DES MISES** before advancing to another question, category, or the end of the game. Wagers settle once and are cleared for subsequent questions and categories. Players see only their own stake while wagering is open.

Every answer reveal displays a leaderboard on the host and TV: the top three teams, plus all teams tied with the third team's score. Scores include the settled wagers. No game-package changes are required.
