Thinking music

A broad first-pass listening list for quiet music under a trivia round. Try the gentler pieces first; the faster or more dramatic works are included as comparisons because they may compete with speech or concentration.

Composer correction: “Morning Mood” and “In the Hall of the Mountain King” are both by Edvard Grieg, from Peer Gynt. Mendelssohn did write a separate piano piece called “Morning Song” and the lively “Spring Song”; both are included here.
