# Inventaire Complet des Sujets et Feuilles (Leaf Subjects)
General comments:
- labels are not meant to differentiate, they are meant to categorize. as such is does not make sense to overspecify them (see comments in tables)
- every answer is not a label. for example "du ciel" is not a label. the label is tied to the question and is Sedna
- unless referring to s specific title (painting, book, etc.) articles like "Un" or "Le" should be removed from labels to keep them short and all-encompassing
- in the same vein of idea, strip gender from labels
- a phrase or a long group of words (unless it's a title) is probably not a label, just an answer to a question associated with a label
- dates and numbers in general are not labels, they are too vague
- charades visuelles require special treatment. the answer is often not a label. for example, "mouiller a siaux" is a quebec expression. labels associated with this would be quebec, french expression, pluie. which also illustrate that a specific answer can be tied to several labels and categories, "dispendieux" would have quebec, vocabulaire as labels, "l'habit ne fait pas le moine" would have expression francaise, moine as label, etc.
- books, painting, etc. typically have a least two labels associated with them author and title
- all labels should be expressed strictly in french to avoid duplications; answers, for example for title, would still allow for the original language version
