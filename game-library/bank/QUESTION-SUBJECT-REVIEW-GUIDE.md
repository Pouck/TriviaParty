# Subject identity review guide

This review is about how subject records relate. It is not a vote on whether questions should be deleted.

For each candidate pair, fill in **identity_decision**:

- `same entity`: consolidate duplicate subject records under one canonical subject. Preserve every valid question and presentation; remove a question only after a separate content review finds it redundant or defective.
- `related entities`: keep separate subject records, add a supported relationship, and consider whether a carefully worded cross-entity question would add value. Existing questions remain.
- `distinct`: keep separate with no relationship supported by the available context.
- `research`: leave unchanged until the identity or relationship is verified.

Then fill **question_action**. The normal choice is `retain all`. Use `review possible duplicate` only when two questions ask the same fact in substantially the same way; it still does not authorize deletion by itself. A new cross-entity question is an additional question, not a replacement for its source questions.

Examples already represented in the batch:

- **Medusa:** mythological figure and a painting titled *Medusa* are different entities with a likely depiction/title relationship. Keep their subjects separate and retain both questions.
- **Notre-Dame de Paris:** Victor Hugo's novel and the cathedral are different entities with a meaningful relationship. Keep them separate and retain both questions.
- **Sunflowers:** the flower and Van Gogh's painting are different entities with a depiction relationship. “Which flowers are in the bouquets?” and “Which painting is this?” can both be valid. Keep both.
- **Michel-Ange / Michelangelo:** if the contexts confirm the same person, consolidate the subject identity while preserving distinct valid questions about him.

The candidate list was generated by label similarity, so suggestions are leads for review, not merge instructions.
