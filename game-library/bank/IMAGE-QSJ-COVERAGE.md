## Image and licensing rules

The current sampler is generated by `build/make-image-qsj-sampler.py` at `build/image-qsj-sampler/sampler.html`. It shows one image at a time and supports BLUR, PIXELATE, SHUFFLE, and UNCOVER. Media source pages, creator credits, and license details are recorded in `IMAGE-QSJ-MEDIA-CREDITS.json`.

A candidate is imported only after its image has been inspected and reuse terms recorded. Reject results that show a different subject, an unrelated interior/detail, answer-bearing lettering, or a materially incomplete view. A painting or a named sculptural group is itself one target, even when the original artwork depicts several figures. Preserve the source artwork as the final reveal image; do not crop away signatures or edit the artwork to make it easier to guess.

## Expansion benchmarks

The painting set tracks [Art & See’s 100 Most Famous Paintings](https://art-and-see.com/pages/famous-paintings); the sculpture discovery set uses [Sculptura’s Top 100 Sculptures](https://www.sculptura.com/top-100-sculptures-history/); building discoveries draw on published icon selections including [DK’s 100 Iconic Buildings](https://dk.com/products/9780241801574-100-iconic-buildings-to-see-before-you-die) and the Modlar collection. These are discovery benchmarks, not a universal ranking. Count coverage from actual matching titles and approved images, not from queued candidates.
